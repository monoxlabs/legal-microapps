Lunaria Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026 · Español

1. Who we are

Lunaria is an app developed by Monoxlabs ("we"). For any privacy question, write to recetasroyale@gmail.com.

2. Data we handle

Lunaria has no user accounts and no servers of its own. We do not collect your name, GPS location, contacts or photos.

The preferences you choose (region or country, language, theme, selections inside the app and whether you bought "Remove ads") are stored only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To serve and measure them, Google may process your device's advertising ID, your IP address, technical device information and your interactions with ads.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before any ad is loaded. If you decline, you may still see non-personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy preferences inside the app.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. More information: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

4. In-app purchases

The "Remove ads" purchase is processed by Google Play. We never receive or store your payment details — only confirmation that the purchase exists, so we can activate and restore it.

5. Ratings

The app may show Google Play's in-app review dialog. Your rating is handled directly by Google Play.

6. If you write to us

"Send a suggestion" opens your email app with a message that includes the app version, operating system, your chosen region and language. We receive your email address and whatever you write, and use it only to reply and to improve the app.

7. Legal basis and retention

We rely on your consent for personalised advertising, and on our legitimate interest in answering you for emails you send us. We keep emails only as long as needed to reply.

8. Children

Lunaria is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability by writing to the email above. You may also lodge a complaint with the data protection authority in your country.

10. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted on the app's Google Play listing.
